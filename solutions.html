GATE 04 SYSTEM ACTIVE

Transforming standard cameras into intelligent damage detection agents. We help logistics companies modernize their container inspection process without replacing existing hardware. Our AI works with your current camera setup to automatically identify and classify damages in real-time. This means faster gate processing, fewer disputes, and better data for your operations team. No manual inspections, no missed damages, just smart automation that saves time and money.

AI Damage Detection

Automated visual inspection of containers from standard camera images. Identifies damaged vs. undamaged containers, detects and classifies damage types according to ISO standards. Removes the need for manual review of undamaged containers and reduces inspection time and operational cost.

Damage Localization & Classification

Precise damage analysis including exact damage location on the container structure, damage extent and affected area, and ISO-compliant damage and repair classification. Ensures consistent, defensible inspection results.

3D Container Visualization

Interactive 3D container models that visualize damage locations spatially on the container. Supports repair planning, audits, and dispute resolution.

Standardized Damage Reporting

Automatically generated inspection reports including damage type and classification, damage location and affected area, high-resolution image evidence, and repair cost estimation. Creates uniform documentation across inspectors, sites, and terminals.

Mobile Inspection App

Mobile solution for manual inspections. Inspect containers using smartphones or tablets with instant generation of ISO-compliant damage reports. Supports yards, depots, and non-gate inspection scenarios.

Software Platform

Central software layer that aggregates inspection data from gate and mobile sources, integrates into existing operational and M&R workflows, and enables digital, traceable container inspection processes.
