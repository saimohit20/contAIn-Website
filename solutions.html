ContAIn Solutions

Transforming standard cameras into intelligent damage detection agents. We help logistics companies modernize their container inspection process without replacing existing hardware. Our AI works with your current camera setup to automatically identify and classify damages in real-time. This means faster gate processing, fewer disputes, and better data for your operations team. No manual inspections, no missed damages, just smart automation that saves time and money.

Gate Automation

Automate processing and validation of truck and container data. Our OCR technology reads container numbers, ISO codes, truck license plates, ILU numbers, yellow plates, and more.

AI Damage Detection

End-to-end solution from container information to damage reporting. Eliminates the need for supervisors to review undamaged containers, saving both time and money.

3D Container Visual

Interactive 3D models to visualize damaged containers. Get a clear spatial understanding of where damages are located on the structure.

Complete Reporting

Comprehensive reports with cost estimation. Includes damage type, repair costs, precise location, damage area calculations, and high-resolution photos.

Web & Mobile App

Intuitive software for inspectors to scan containers and generate reports instantly using smartphones or tablets.

Automation Software

We provide essential data as well as automation software applications empowering the digital transformation of container handling processes.
