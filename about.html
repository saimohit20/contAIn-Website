About us

contAIn was born when three students during their master’s studies recognized that traditional container monitoring couldn't keep pace with an increasingly digital world. What global logistics through intelligence.

Our solution goal is to provide the precision needed to secure and monitor the world’s supply chains, making it possible for ports and operators to work with more confidence and clarity than ever before.
