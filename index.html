Why Choose contAIn?

We bring cutting-edge computer vision to logistics, ensuring no damage goes unnoticed. Our platform integrates seamlessly with your existing infrastructure, delivering enterprise-grade accuracy without disrupting daily operations.

AI Damage Detection

Automated visual inspection identifies damaged vs. undamaged containers and classifies damages according to ISO standards in real-time.

Damage Localization

Precise analysis including exact damage location on the structure, damage extent, and ISO-compliant repair classification.

3D Visualization

Interactive 3D container models that visualize damage locations spatially, supporting repair planning and dispute resolution.

Standardized Reporting

Automatically generated inspection reports with high-resolution evidence, mapping coordinates, and complete cost estimations.

Mobile Inspection App

Mobile solution for manual inspections with instant generation of ISO-compliant reports on smartphones or tablets.

Software Platform

Central software layer integrating gate and mobile data into your existing operational and M&R workflows.
